// about me

Nithin Palegar

I'm Nithin — a final-year B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student at GM University, and a practicing ethical hacker focused on offensive security. My primary areas of work are web application security, vulnerability research, and bug bounty hunting.

Over the course of my studies I have identified and responsibly disclosed security vulnerabilities to organisations including NASA, WHO, LG Electronics, ISRO, and UIDAI. I work within strict ethical and legal frameworks and believe in transparent communication with security teams throughout the disclosure process.

My current research interest is in LLM Security — exploring how Large Language Models can be tested, stressed, and hardened against adversarial inputs and misuse. I'm also continuing to develop open-source tools for the bug bounty community.

I write about my findings and methodology on Medium, covering topics like SQL injection, WAF bypass techniques, and broader web security research.
